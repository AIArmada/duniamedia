# Homepage image art direction

Homepage photography is chosen by job and crop, with a deliberately mixed palette. Generated campaign scenes are illustrative; company marks remain genuine supplied logo assets.

| Placement | Asset | Visual role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desktop hero | `hero-media-city.webp` | Cinematic Kuala Lumpur at coral dusk; landmarks and media sit at the sides while the calmer center supports the headline. |
| Mobile hero | `hero-media-mobile.webp` | A separate vertical composition keeps the skyline and side screens in frame instead of cropping the desktop panorama. |
| About feature | `city.webp` | Bright, grounded strategy session with a local team and a clear Kuala Lumpur setting. |
| TV & broadcast channel | `tv.webp` | Warm, daylight newsroom with an anchor and camera crew; editorial and credible without the former electric-blue wash. |
| Outdoor channel | `outdoor.webp` | Loud golden-hour billboard and commuter traffic; the category needs immediate scale and impact. |
| Radio channel | `radio.webp` | Warm human studio portrait retained because it already communicates radio naturally. |
| Digital channel | `digital.webp` | Bright tabletop and phone scene retained for its clean, current and approachable feel. |
| Print channel | `print.webp` | Restrained pressroom machinery retained as a tactile, quieter counterpoint. |
| In-premise channel | `indoor.webp` | Daylit mall architecture retained for its warm, open sense of place. |
| Creator channel | `creators.webp` | Calm, natural-light creator portrait retained to represent a working creator without a staged event look. |
| Training promo | `training.webp` | Bright practical workshop, focused on people collaborating rather than a blue conference stage. |
| Creator promo | `creator-feature.webp` | Separate close portrait retained for a stylish, personal creator-network moment. |
| Custom-media promo | `custom-media.webp` | Immersive coral-and-magenta architectural installation, used as the page's vivid campaign accent. |
| Featured media cards | `listing-*.webp` | Six separate scenes show the specific placement in context, avoiding repeats of the channel strip. |
| Insights cards | `editorial-*.webp` | Three distinct, quieter editorial photographs keep the lower page legible and avoid reusing channel art. |

Wide cards use 16:9 WebP derivatives; promo tiles use square derivatives; the About feature uses a 4:3 image; hero art has independent 3:1 desktop and 4:5 mobile compositions. Existing well-matched channel images were kept rather than regenerated for the sake of consistency.
