## Typeset marks

These names remain text rather than using unrelated, guessed or unverified artwork: JWP, Digital Malaysia, Imperia Asia Resources, Hana Medic, Maxxima Resources, R Rayyan, MK Label, i Secret and MASYUK.

JWP's public resource page publishes the KWP ministry logo and agency assets but no separate JWP logo, so the gallery typesets its official agency name and keeps it distinct from KWP. Digital Malaysia is kept distinct from the later Malaysia Digital initiative: [MDEC's brand update](https://www.mdec.my/static/pdf/malaysiadigital/Malaysia%20Digital%20Brand%20Updates.pdf) documents the separate Malaysia Digital identity. The listed `maxxima.my` domain currently serves unrelated content, so it was not used as a logo source. MyCC's redesigned butterfly logo is taken from its own public site, which also records the rebrand: [MyCC logo announcement](https://www.mycc.gov.my/announcement/myccs-logo-reimagined-redesigned-refined).
