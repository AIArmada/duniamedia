# Dunia Media

Dunia Media is a media and advertising marketplace. This glossary fixes the language used across the public site, admin, seed data and API.

## Classification

**Platform**:
The broad delivery world. Traditional covers broadcast, print, outdoor and cinema. Digital covers websites, apps, social, OTT and digital screens.
_Avoid_: Channel, medium

**Medium**:
The type of media, such as television, radio, print, out-of-home, DOOH, website, social media, creator, cinema or place-based media.
_Avoid_: Media category, channel, type

The catalogue currently uses these medium labels: TV & Broadcast, Radio, Podcast, Outdoor, Indoor, Digital, Print and Creators. In the wider industry map, DOOH is a subset of Outdoor, Website and Social Media are subsets of Digital, and Cinema and Place-Based & Custom are additional categories.

**Media Category / Media Type**:
The catalogue category for a medium, such as TV, radio, print, OOH, DOOH, website, social, creator, cinema or place-based. Prefer **Medium** in customer-facing language and **Media Category** in taxonomy or admin.
_Avoid_: Media group, platform

**Media Group**:
The internal grouping above a media category, such as broadcast, audio, out-of-home, digital, print, creator or events.
_Avoid_: Media category

**Channel**:
The specific distribution outlet or brand a buyer appears on, such as TV3, Hot FM, 988, BFM, The Star or a named YouTube channel.
_Avoid_: Medium, outlet, vendor

**Ad Format**:
The shape of an ad placement, such as a 30-second spot, billboard face, lift-screen loop, Instagram Story or sponsored article. Use **Format** in customer-facing language.
_Avoid_: Placement, medium

## Supply and rights

**Media Owner**:
The organisation that owns or controls a media property.
_Avoid_: Supplier, vendor, publisher

**Media Operator**:
The organisation that runs a media asset or network day to day, including installation, playback and maintenance.
_Avoid_: Media owner

**Media Rights Holder / Concessionaire**:
The party that holds the lease, concession or licence to operate or sell a media property.
_Avoid_: Media owner, unless the same party is both

**Media Network Developer**:
The party that builds a network of media assets or channels.
_Avoid_: Media owner

**Media Sales House / Representative**:
The party that sells inventory on behalf of one or more media owners.
_Avoid_: Media owner, agency

**Site Acquisition Company**:
The party that identifies and secures locations for media development.
_Avoid_: Media owner

**Installer / System Integrator**:
The party that installs and integrates the physical or digital media system.
_Avoid_: Media operator

**Publisher**:
The party that produces editorial content and may also own a media property.
_Avoid_: Media owner, unless the same party is both

**Media Property**:
The owned entity behind a channel, such as a television station, radio station, publication, website, social account or physical network.
_Avoid_: Channel, media asset

**Media Asset**:
The physical or digital object that carries advertising, such as a billboard, screen, poster frame, table display or website container.
_Avoid_: Media property, placement

**Ad Surface**:
The physical or digital surface on a media asset that can carry creative, such as a billboard face, screen panel or lift loop.
_Avoid_: Media asset, placement

**Ad Placement**:
The bookable opportunity formed by an ad surface or property, a format and inventory. A billboard face, a TV break, a website banner or an Instagram Story is an ad placement.
_Avoid_: Medium, format, media offering

**Media Offering**:
The commercial product a seller presents, packaging one or more ad placements with a unit and a rate.
_Avoid_: Ad placement, rate card

**Media Opportunity**:
The customer-facing name for a sellable media offering or ad placement in the catalogue.
_Avoid_: Listing, inventory

**Seller**:
The party that issues a media offering. A seller is usually the media owner or a media sales house.
_Avoid_: Supplier, vendor

**Rate Card**:
The seller's published pricing reference for a media offering or group of offerings.
_Avoid_: Quote, invoice

**Package**:
A bundled set of media offerings sold together, often with a package saving.
_Avoid_: Campaign plan

**Inventory**:
The available capacity for an ad placement, measured in slots, quantity, time or impressions.
_Avoid_: Media offering, rate card

**Location**:
The umbrella term for a venue or a site. Use **Venue** or **Site** when the distinction matters.
_Avoid_: Venue, site

**Venue**:
A location with an audience, such as a mall, restaurant, office or hospital.
_Avoid_: Site, media property

**Site**:
A specific piece of land, roadside, building or structure used for media.
_Avoid_: Venue, location

**Host Structure**:
The structure that carries a media asset, such as a pillar, wall, lift, table, vehicle or gantry.
_Avoid_: Media asset, site

## Demand and campaigns

**Buyer / Client (Advertiser)**:
The organisation that commissions and pays for a campaign. When an agency buys on behalf of a brand, the brand is the advertiser and the agency is the buyer.
_Avoid_: Customer

**Agency**:
The organisation that plans, buys or manages media on behalf of a buyer.
_Avoid_: Media sales house

**Brand**:
The commercial identity being advertised.
_Avoid_: Client, product

**Product**:
A specific item or service being advertised.
_Avoid_: Brand

**Service**:
A capability delivered by Dunia Media, such as media planning, creative production, AI training or campaign measurement.
_Avoid_: Product

**Campaign Plan**:
A saved working mix of media offerings, quantities, package savings and brief metadata. It is not a booking.
_Avoid_: Cart, order, campaign

**Enquiry**:
A request or brief from a buyer before a quote exists.
_Avoid_: Lead, campaign plan

**Quote / Proposal**:
The commercial response to an enquiry, with priced lines and validity. A proposal is the customer-facing form of a quote.
_Avoid_: Booking, order

**Booking**:
A confirmed reservation of inventory against a quote.
_Avoid_: Quote, order

**Campaign**:
The execution of one or more booked ad placements for a client, including flights, creative, delivery and results.
_Avoid_: Campaign plan, project

**Flight**:
A scheduled period within a campaign.
_Avoid_: Campaign

**Delivery**:
The recorded fulfilment of a flight placement, such as an aired spot, played loop or published post.
_Avoid_: Performance metric

**Performance Metric**:
A measured outcome or delivery number, such as reach, impressions, clicks, enquiries or sales.
_Avoid_: Delivery

**Case Study**:
A published, verified account of a project or campaign and its results.
_Avoid_: Work, testimonial

**Testimonial**:
A client statement about the work, with consent and visibility recorded.
_Avoid_: Case study

**Work / History**:
The archive of past campaigns and client engagements.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, case study

## People and content

**Creator**:
A person or small team that produces audience-led content.
_Avoid_: Media owner, publisher

**Influencer**:
A creator whose primary value is audience reach and persuasion.
_Avoid_: Content creator, media owner

**Content Creator**:
A creator whose primary value is production craft, such as video, photo or editorial content.
_Avoid_: Influencer

**Social Media Channel**:
A creator's or publisher's account on a social platform, such as TikTok, Instagram or YouTube.
_Avoid_: Medium, media property

## Terms to avoid

- **Listing**: use media opportunity, media property or ad placement depending on the object.
- **Supplier**: use media owner or media sales house.
- **Vendor**: use channel or media owner.
- **Outlet**: use channel in customer-facing language.
- **Order**: use booking for media inventory.
- **Customer**: use buyer or client.
- **Media type**: use medium for TV/radio/print and media category for the catalogue grouping.
- **Ad**: use ad placement, format or creative depending on what is meant.
